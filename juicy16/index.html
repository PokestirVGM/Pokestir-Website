Juicy16

A free DLS and SoundFont plugin for Mac and Windows. I made Juicy16 to play game MIDI in my DAW, much like Fruity LSD. Load the sound bank that goes with your MIDI and each channel gets its own instrument.

How it works

Juicy16 loads DLS, SF2 and SF3 banks. Your MIDI file picks the instruments for all 16 channels, and you can adjust their volume, pan, mute and solo in the plugin.

Everything comes out as one stereo mix, with reverb and chorus if you want them. You’ll need your own sound banks and a DAW to play the MIDI.

This update improves stereo DLS panning and fixes some silent drum kits. There’s also an interpolation setting for changing how samples sound when pitched up or down.

Getting started

  1. Run the Windows installer, or unzip the Mac download and open install_macos.command. Restart your DAW and rescan plugins if Juicy16 doesn’t appear.
  2. Add Juicy16 as an instrument and load your DLS, SF2 or SF3 bank.
  3. Route your MIDI to it, keeping the original channels, and play from the beginning so the instrument changes are picked up.
