Using My Music

Content ID Claims

Claims on covers are automated matches, not strikes against you. If YouTube flagged your video, here's how to sort it out.

Incorrect Content ID claims

I license my covers legally through my label, so please don't worry if you see a claim. Claims naming Materia Music Publishing or FUGA are automated Content ID matches, not manual claims from me.

Even if you didn't use my recording, YouTube can still match the shared melody of a game piece. It's worth disputing the claim; it should be resolved fairly quickly.

FAQ

Is a claim a strike?

No, not at all. Content ID claims only affect monetization, not your channel status, and they're fully automated.

Why did it match my video?

Covers share melodies with the original game music, so the system can match your video even if you used a different recording.

Steps if you received a claim

  1. Please check the claim text first. If it names Materia Music Publishing, FUGA, or one of my cover tracks, it's an automated Content ID match. It was not manually made by me or my distributor.
  2. Please dispute the claim in YouTube Studio using this statement:
    No audio from the claimed recording is present; the match is incorrect.
  3. Give it 5 to 7 days. Most claims lift automatically, and monetization along with any held revenue should return.
  4. If you'd like, you're also welcome to use YouTube Studio's tools to remove the matched audio.
